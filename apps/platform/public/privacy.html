Prototype privacy information

Architect is an independent working hiring-assignment prototype operated by Darsh Dave, not the official Architect or Lyzr service. Last updated: 27 September 2026.

What this prototype stores

Signing in through Google shares your name, email address and profile information with Supabase Authentication. Your account’s projects, prompts, documents, generated source and configuration drafts are saved in a Supabase database. Authentication session information is stored in your browser. The demo uses local browser storage.

AI and hosting providers

When you request live AI generation or review, the prompt and relevant project context or attachments are sent through OpenRouter to the chosen available free model provider. Provider handling is governed by their own policies. Vercel hosts the app and may process request and operational logs. Do not enter confidential business information, credentials or sensitive personal data into this prototype.

Your choices

You can use the demo without creating an account, export projects, delete projects and sign out. Demo data can also be removed by clearing this site’s browser storage. For account or stored-data deletion, contact the operator below. Deleting a project does not necessarily erase historical backups or provider logs immediately.
